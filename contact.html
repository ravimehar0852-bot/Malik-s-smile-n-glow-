FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the treatment painful?

We use modern, gentle protocols including local anaesthesia where needed, so most patients describe our treatments — including root canals — as pain-free or minimally uncomfortable.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

Appointments are recommended so we can plan your visit properly, but walk-ins are welcome — we'll accommodate you as soon as a slot opens.

What are your working hours?

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Sunday appointments can be arranged on request.

Do you offer both dental and cosmetic treatments in one visit?

Yes — Dr. Malik is a Dental Surgeon and Medical Cosmetologist, so many patients combine a dental treatment with a facial aesthetic session in the same visit.

How can I book an appointment?

You can call us, message us on WhatsApp, or fill the appointment form on this page — our team will confirm your slot promptly.
